📦 Inventory & Supply Chain Management Analytics Dashboard

1. Recommended Structure & Headline
Repository Headline
End-to-End Supply Chain & Inventory Intelligence: Stock Level Optimization, OTIF Order Fulfillment, Supplier Lead Time Benchmarking, and Carrying Cost Analytics

2. Short Description & Purpose
Overview
The Inventory & Supply Chain Management Analytics Dashboard is an enterprise BI solution built in Microsoft Power BI designed to bridge procurement, warehouse operations, and outbound logistics. It translates raw operational tables from ERP and WMS databases into high-impact operational intelligence.
Business Problem Solved
Supply chain leaders frequently face conflicting trade-offs: holding excess safety stock drives up carrying costs and risk of obsolescence, while running lean inventories leads to stockouts, delayed fulfillment, and churned customers. This dashboard delivers continuous visibility across the order life-cycle, balancing inventory health against on-time delivery commitments.
 #Core Objectives
●	Optimize Working Capital: Monitor inventory carrying costs, turnover rates, and deadstock levels across warehouses and product lines.
●	Mitigate Stockout Risk: Flag SKUs dipping below minimum safety thresholds and forecast replenishment requirements based on dynamic lead times.
●	Benchmark Supplier Performance: Track supplier on-time delivery rates, fulfillment variance, defect rates, and vendor lead times.
●	Enhance Logistics Reliability: Measure customer order fulfillment performance through industry-standard OTIF (On-Time, In-Full) scores.

4. Features & Key Highlights
1. Executive Inventory Scorecard
●	Total Inventory Value: Real-time visibility into capital tied up in stock on hand.
●	Inventory Turnover Ratio (ITR): High-level efficiency indicator demonstrating how rapidly inventory is sold and replaced over time.
●	Days Sales of Inventory (DSI): Measures the average number of days required to turn inventory into revenue.
2. Reorder Alerting & Safety Stock Monitoring
●	Dynamic Stock Level Flags: Automatic classification of products into Normal Stock, Under-Stocked / Reorder Needed, and Critical Out of Stock.
●	Lead-Time Risk Analysis: Reorder calculations factoring in historical vendor delivery variance against contracted lead times.
3. Order Fulfillment & OTIF Tracking
●	On-Time In-Full (OTIF) Rate: Dual-condition KPI verifying both delivery timeliness against the customer's promised SLA and complete quantity delivery.
●	Backorder & Shortage Index: Identifies lines with high cancellation and backorder occurrences to prevent service degradation.
4. Vendor & Inbound Logistics Performance
●	Supplier Lead Time Variance: Comparison between scheduled PO delivery dates versus receipt at dock door.
●	Defect & Rejection Rates: Track quality compliance on incoming raw goods or commercial merchandise before staging.
5. Warehouse Utilization & Carrying Costs
●	Storage Footprint: Real-time capacity utilization tracking by physical warehouse location to avoid overflow fees.
●	Holding Cost Breakdown: Calculation of financing, storage, insurance, and shrinkage expenses by product category.
